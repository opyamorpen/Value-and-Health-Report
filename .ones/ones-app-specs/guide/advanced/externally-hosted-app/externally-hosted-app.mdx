# Externally Hosted App

:::info
Most developers should build apps as **ONES-Hosted Apps**. See **[Getting Started](../../getting-started/getting-started.mdx)**.

Externally hosted apps are only recommended for specific scenarios, such as when you use ONES Cloud but still need to deploy your app on your own infrastructure.
:::

## What Is an Externally Hosted App?

An Externally Hosted App is an app that runs on your own infrastructure.

Instead of packaging your app as a `.opkx` file and uploading it to ONES, you deploy your application on your own servers and provide ONES with a publicly accessible URL.

## When to Use Externally Hosted Apps

Externally hosted apps are suitable for the following scenarios:

- **Custom Infrastructure**: You have existing infrastructure and want to deploy ONES apps alongside other services
- **Network Requirements**: Your app needs to be deployed in a specific network environment (e.g., internal network, VPN)
- **Compliance Requirements**: Your organization requires apps to run on-premises or in specific regions
- **Integration Needs**: Your app needs to integrate with local services or databases that cannot be accessed from ONES-hosted infrastructure

## Differences from ONES-Hosted Apps

| Aspect                 | ONES-Hosted App             | Externally Hosted App      |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| **Deployment**         | Upload `.opkx` file to ONES | Deploy on your own servers |
| **Infrastructure**     | Managed by ONES             | Managed by you             |
| **Development**        | Develop with `ones` CLI     | Use any web framework      |
| **Scaling**            | Handled by ONES             | Your responsibility        |
| **ONES Cloud Support** | No                          | Yes                        |
